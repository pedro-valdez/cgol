    Grid

    1. The game takes palce on a grid of cells. A cell has 8 neighbors.

    2. The cells in gray are the neighbors of the cell in white.

  • ❯
    ❮

    Rule of Survival

    1. A cell with 2 or 3 live neighbors survives.

    2. The cell in the middle survives.

  • ❯
    ❮

    Rule of Abundance

    1. A dead cell with 3 live neighbors revives.

    2. The cell in the middle revives.

  • ❯
    ❮

    Rule of Overabundance

    1. A cell with more than 3 neighbors dies.

    2. The cell in the middle dies.

  • ❯
    ❮

    Rule of Underabundance

    1. A cell with less than 2 dies.

    2. The cell in the middle dies.
